# Chengyu gap shortlist (phase 4)

Important chengyu that were not yet in the dictionary. They're ranked by how many sources agree,
how important the reviewers rated them, and how often they turn up in a second frequency source
(the jieba word list) and in THUOCL ranks 5,001–8,519.

**Sources:** 3,900 candidates from the Xinhua idiom dictionary and THUOCL, sorted by reviewers,
plus lists of story/fable/textbook idioms and exam/misused/everyday idioms.
Longer sayings (e.g. 纸里包不住火) moved to the 俗语 phase. Other spellings of existing entries
(499) were merged into those entries instead of being added. Forms in （ ） will be listed as
"Also written" on the new entry.

Plan: write entries for **1–1,000**. Tell me anything to drop, or anything from the reserve
(1,001–1,300) to move up.
