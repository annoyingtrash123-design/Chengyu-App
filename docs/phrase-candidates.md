# 四字词语 candidates (not chengyu, but often unfamiliar to learners)

**Scope agreed:** narrative & descriptive phrases, cultural sets & terms, and common
news/formal phrases (tagged `formal`). Not included: written connectors (也就是说…) and
AABB reduplications. Amount: as many as are useful — keep going down the jieba frequency
list until what's left is too technical or too obvious.

Checked against chinese-xinhua (so none of these are already classed as 成语) and the jieba
frequency dictionary. Candidates that turned out to be chengyu (琴棋书画, 车水马龙, 一言不发…)
will come in with the chengyu instead, so nothing is duplicated.
