# 语林词典 — adversarial review, November 2026

Review of v0.23 (11,852 entries) following `docs/review-plan.md`. 19 review streams: 4 user personas, 3 break-it
testers, 3 code reviewers, 3 content samplers (412 random entries), accessibility, usability, speed, platforms,
competitors, and publishing readiness. Run by Opus (lead) with Sonnet and Haiku agents; every S1/S2 finding below was
reproduced by the lead or independently by two agents. **Nothing was changed during the review.**

About 230 findings were recorded; after removing duplicates and invalid ones they group into the themes below.
29 + 25 "star rating" findings from two content agents were **withdrawn**: the agents read the frequency field
backwards (1 = ★★★). Re-checked, the star ratings in the sample are sound.

## Verdict in one paragraph

The core is solid: search by characters, pinyin, variants and traditional characters works; scheduling maths, dates
and time zones are correct; offline use works fully after one load; no console errors anywhere; the privacy statement
matches what the app does. The serious problems are **(1) there is no way to back up progress**, so one cleared
browser or one APK reinstall loses everything; **(2) a handful of ways progress can silently fail to save**; and
**(3) content polish at scale**: about 195 entries show broken English placeholders, and synonym/antonym lists are
often padded, loose or occasionally vulgar. After that come English search ranking, a few game/study bugs, beginner
guidance, accessibility and publishing preparation.

## Findings by theme

### 1. Backup and data safety — S1
- No export/import/backup control in the web app or the APK. The core API exists (`api.exportData`/`importData`)
  but nothing calls it (B2-2, C2-3, A2-1, G1-1). Verified by the lead.
- Every CI build signs the APK with a fresh debug key, so a new APK cannot install over an old one; users must
  uninstall, which deletes `progress.json` (I1-1, G1-1).
- Release notes still advertise backups and "Recently viewed / Studied" lists that were removed (C2-3b, A1-7).
- **Fix:** add Export / Import (file) to Me, with a "last backed up" date and a gentle reminder; sign the APK with one
  fixed key (needs your approval — see questions); correct the release notes.

### 2. Saving can silently fail — S2
- Saved data is restored through the same check as a backup import, which rejects data with no cards, favourites,
  tags or history: changing only settings (e.g. English off) is lost on every reload, and each reload writes another
  "unreadable" copy to storage (C2-1, verified).
- Storage full or blocked: the heart turns red but nothing is saved, with no warning (B2-3, C2-4).
- Two tabs: removing the last favourite/tag in one tab is undone by the other (C2-2); settings from a stale tab
  overwrite newer ones (C2-7).
- Android: an unreadable `progress.json` is silently replaced by empty data (C2-5); a failed disk write leaves memory
  and disk out of step (C2-6). A reminder set for 00:xx fires at 20:xx (C2-8).
- **Fix:** separate "restore saved state" from "import backup"; show a toast when a save fails; merge on the
  cross-tab storage event; keep a safety copy on Android; fix `hh || 20`.

### 3. Content defects at scale — S2
- Placeholders: 95 entries with `'{}'`, 100 with `'§'` in English examples, all from the October 名句/诗句 batches
  (D3-1, D2-29, A3-6; verified). Users see e.g. *"She quoted '{}' to say she would not give up."*
- Quiz leak: 170 entries' English examples contain the Chinese headword, which the fill-the-sentence question shows
  under the blank (A1-3; verified).
- **Fix:** regenerate those English examples with the quoted line translated; in the quiz, mask the headword in the
  translation or hide it.

### 4. Synonym and antonym lists — S2
- Padding: ≈400 entries have 12+ antonyms and ≈170 have 12+ synonyms; 1,062 chips are longer than 12 characters
  (quotations); many antonyms are merely different (喜出望外 ≠ 万箭穿心, 高枕无忧 ≠ 厉兵秣马) (A3-1, D2-35, D3-5).
  This mostly predates the 11 Nov two-way completion (382 → 406 entries with 12+ antonyms).
- Two forms of one expression in the same list: 25 lists by strict matching, ≈220 counting short form + full quote
  (A3-7, A4-6, D3-3, D3-4, D2-40/41).
- Vulgar sayings (屎/尿/茅坑/放屁) appear on 46 otherwise clean entries, e.g. 埋头苦干, 画蛇添足 (A4-2, A3-4; verified).
- Demeaning or loaded items: 小脚女人 as an antonym (D1-19); 存天理，灭人欲 as a synonym (D3-8).
- **Fix:** cap and rank lists (suggest ≤ 6 each, best first), drop quotations from link lists unless the entry is
  itself a quotation, remove vulgar items from non-colloquial entries, dedupe forms. A pass by agents plus your rule.

### 5. Search — S2
- English: edit-distance-1 matches score the same as exact words ("light" → *eight* → 半斤八两); pinyin matches
  cross syllable boundaries and outrank English ("run" → rú+niǎo, "man" → 蛮, "go" → gōng) (B1-1, B1-3, A1-1, C3-3;
  verified on the live app).
- A pasted sentence containing an idiom finds nothing (B1-2, C3-4); ü typed as u fails for short queries ("nu wa",
  "lu dong bin") (B1-14, B1-4); full-width Latin finds nothing (B1-5).
- **Fix:** exact English word > prefix > fuzzy; pinyin matching on syllable boundaries only; find idioms inside a
  pasted sentence; treat u as ü where it makes a valid syllable.

### 6. Labels — S2
- 勤奋敬业 (under 品德性格) lacks the core diligence idioms, which carry only the broad 志向努力; it also holds
  off-topic entries (以身殉职, 目不斜视); the map shows 懒惰 under 志向努力 but no diligence node (A4-1, A4-7; verified).
- Single mismatches in the sample: 癞蛤蟆想吃天鹅肉 labelled polite-humble (D1-15), 上无片瓦… as poor clothing
  (D1-14), 天造地设 / 黄钟毁弃 / 十指不沾阳春水 (D3-16/17/19).
- **Fix:** a label-tree audit for overlapping concepts across broad groups, then back-fill the detailed label.

### 7. Study and games — S2/S3
- Study: after leaving a session (or opening "Full entry" and returning), keys 1–4 still grade the old card
  (C3-1, B1-12; reproduced twice). Enter/Space act on Study even when a header button has focus (C3-2).
- Variants rejected: chain (1,232 entries have variants with different first/last characters), unscramble (390
  reordered variants), crossword typing (traditional input) (C3-5, B1-10, C3-6, C3-7, B1-11).
- Quiz: two valid answers (三五成群 / 三三两两) (A2-3); synonym match 0.6% ambiguous boards (C3-8); repeated
  questions on small decks (A2-4, B1-9); "Show answers" in crossword records every word as wrong (C3-7).
- Timed challenge counts ticks, so a sleeping phone pauses the clock (B1-8, C3-9). Pinyin quiz uses citation tones
  for 一/不, which can make the spoken reading a "wrong" option (C3-10).

### 8. Beginners and usability — S2/S3
- No help or onboarding; terms never defined; badges not tappable (E2-1). Search page is blank (E2-2).
- With English on, list badges (成语, 褒义, 学习中) and synonym chips are Chinese-only (A1-2, A1-9); two chips both
  read "Neutral" (tone 中性, register 通用) (A1-8).
- Study shows "0 cards ready" after the daily new-card limit with no explanation; "New cards per day" accepts 0
  silently (A2-5, B1-6, E2-7). Quiz defaults to every type and classical couplets (A1-4). New cards were
  alphabetical (**fixed 12 Nov**: now most common first within each star tier).
- Smaller: Pattern filter is ~70 raw codes (A2-2); Labels vs My tags both called 标签 (E2-4); Study page title
  differs from its tab (E2-5); errors shown raw ("出错了 … unknown entry") (B2-4, E2-3); no report-a-mistake route
  although About asks for reports (E2-11).

### 9. Accessibility — S2/S3
- Inactive tab labels 3.6:1 (light) / 4.1:1 (dark) at 11 px (E1-1); focus outline removed on crossword clues and
  Home links (E1-4); light-mode badges and the gold source line below 4.5:1 (E1-2, E1-3).
- Font sizes are in px, so the phone's large-text setting barely changes anything; at 200% zoom entry headings are
  cut off and several pages scroll sideways (B3-X2, B3-G1…G4).
- Unnamed buttons (crossword cells, scramble slots), an empty `h1` on entries, Home's dots marked as a tablist,
  map bubbles focusable inside an image, 9 Tab presses from search to the first result (E1-5…E1-11).
- Tap targets 25–36 px on chips, links and crossword cells (B3-G5…G9).
- Page-wide pinch zoom is locked **by your decision** (stops the jiggle); the mind map keeps its own pinch zoom. Not
  treated as a defect.

### 10. Sharing, teaching and export — S2/S3
- No share, copy, CSV or Anki export of an entry or a list (H1-3, A4-4).
- Export PDF: the title ignores stars and My tags ("All entries 全部词条"), the sheet has no examples, and
  `window.print()` probably does nothing in the Android app (A4-3, C1-6, G1-2).

### 11. Publishing readiness — S2/S3
- Google Play needs a hosted privacy-policy URL, a developer contact, a signed release AAB; the APK is an
  unoptimised debug build, arm64 only, and always reports version 0.1.0 (I1-2, G1-4).
- The app id `com.annoyingtrash123.chengyu` becomes permanent on first Play release (G1-9, I1-2).
- Licence texts missing for OpenCC (Apache-2.0) and CC-CEDICT (CC BY-SA); 458 Rust and npm packages uncredited; no
  LICENSE file (I1-3, I1-4). Android CSP is null (I1-7). Reminders stop after 7 days unless the app is opened (G1-6).
- About 10 current-leader quotations and party slogans appear as ordinary 名句/俗语 with no context (I1-6) — needs
  your decision.

### 12. Speed and first load — S3
- A first visit downloads ≈5.6 MB (the dictionary is already compressed, so the server can't shrink it); on a slow
  connection (200 KB/s) the screen is blank for ≈39 s with no loading indicator (B1-15). After that it works offline.
  F1 measurements: see the addendum.

### 13. Content accuracy (sample of 412 entries)
About **16 %** of sampled entries have at least one issue (D1 21/138, D2 ≈25/137 after removing invalid star
findings, D3 20/137). By type, roughly: synonym/antonym 7 %, examples/English 3 %, labels 2 %, origins 1 % of entries
(≈3 % of entries that have an origin), meanings 0.5 %, pinyin 0.2 %. Meanings and pinyin are reliable; links are not.
Concrete errors found include:
- 坦坦荡荡: the Chinese origin "quote" is a modern sentence, not the Analects line 君子坦荡荡，小人长戚戚 (verified).
- 正入万山圈子里: the set has six poems, not five; the poem ends 一山放出一山拦, not "mountains playing games" (verified).
- 悬梁刺股: origin cites 《汉书》 for Sun Jing (medium confidence).
- Garbled or ungrammatical examples: 神女有心，襄王无梦 (D1-17), 人为刀俎，我为鱼肉 (D1-22), 一根绳拴俩蚂蚱,
  腊月的萝卜 (D3-14/15); 丈二和尚 "ten-foot" vs twelve (D2-30); headword 九千九百岁 vs standard 九千岁 (D2-33 note).

### 14. Navigation and UI details — S3/S4
- Unknown links (#/bogus) trap Back on Home (B2-1, C1-4). A deleted or renamed My tag leaves an un-untickable List
  filter (B2-5, C1-7); tag names have no length limit and can push Rename/Delete off screen (B2-6, E2-8).
- Shrinking the window (split screen, desktop) hides the tab bar as if a keyboard opened (C1-5).
- Map: double-tap zooms twice; Back after a reload inside the map needs three presses; no re-layout on rotation;
  "其他" count differs from the List (C1-1, C1-2, C1-10, C1-3).
- Peek sheet: no Escape, no focus trap (C1-9); EN toggle leaves tab labels in the old language until next screen
  (C1-11). Android rename-tag dialog opens empty; dialog buttons always English (G1-3).

## What is clearly good (keep it)
Bilingual entries with literal and natural English, 褒/贬 and register; 歇后语/俗语 coverage beyond most
competitors; SRS study plus eight games; the contents map and labels; fully offline with no ads, accounts or
tracking; correct scheduling and dates; traditional-character and variant search.

## Not tested
No Android device (dialogs, print, notifications, signing were judged from source), no iOS Safari, no screen
reader, no human native speaker. Content judgements are from AI agents with lead spot-checks.

## Suggested fix order
1. **Data safety:** export/import UI, restore-path fix, save-failure warning, two-tab merge, Android safety copy,
   fixed signing key, correct release notes.
2. **Content at scale:** placeholders, quiz leak, link-list pruning and caps, vulgar items, the specific errors above.
3. **Correctness:** English search ranking, study key handler, variant-aware games, ambiguous quiz answers, timed clock.
4. **Beginners and accessibility:** short onboarding + tappable term definitions, English labels on chips/badges,
   Study dead end, contrast, focus, rem-based text sizes, empty-state hints.
5. **Sharing and publishing:** copy/share/CSV, better PDF, Play prerequisites, licences.
